# Lala-Islamzadeh
SynX is an AI-powered human safety platform designed for real-time biometric monitoring, predictive health analytics, and emergency response automation. The system combines wearable sensors, AI, and cloud infrastructure to support proactive healthcare, early risk detection, and protection for vulnerable populations.
SynX is an AI-powered human safety platform focused on transforming healthcare from reactive treatment into proactive life protection. The system is designed to continuously monitor real-time biometric signals, predict potential health risks through artificial intelligence, and automate emergency response processes before critical medical events occur.

The platform combines wearable bio-integrated sensors, machine learning algorithms, cloud infrastructure, and predictive analytics into a unified human safety ecosystem. SynX is capable of analyzing heart activity, oxygen saturation, movement patterns, and other physiological indicators in real time to detect anomalies and provide early warnings for individuals, caregivers, and healthcare providers.

Our mission is to build scalable AI-powered safety infrastructure that can improve healthcare accessibility, emergency readiness, and preventive monitoring, especially for vulnerable populations such as children, elderly individuals, and people living in underserved or remote regions.

By integrating intelligent monitoring technologies with automated response systems, SynX aims to reduce preventable deaths, support healthcare systems with real-time data, and establish a next-generation global standard for human safety and proactive digital healthcare.
